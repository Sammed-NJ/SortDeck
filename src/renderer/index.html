Troubleshoot

Check for problems

Checks the settings file, activity log, watched folders, start with Windows and the Start menu shortcut. Repair only appears if something is broken.

If SortDeck won't open at all, download and run the installer again. It repairs the app and keeps your folders and rules.

Uninstall

Uninstall SortDeck

Removes the app. Your folders, rules and settings are kept in case you reinstall. Your files stay where they are.

Uninstall and delete all data

Removes the app and everything it stored: watched folders, rules, settings and history. Cannot be undone. Your files stay where they are.
